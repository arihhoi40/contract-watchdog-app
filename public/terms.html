What Contract Watchdog does

Contract Watchdog analyzes contracts you submit — as pasted text or photos — and returns automated observations about clauses that may be risky, unusual, or missing standard protections.

This is not legal advice. Contract Watchdog is an automated tool, not a lawyer, and its output should not be relied on as a substitute for review by a qualified attorney. For anything with real money, liability, or risk on the line, have a professional review the contract.

Your account

You need an account (email + password) to use the service. You're responsible for keeping your login credentials secure and for any activity under your account. The free plan includes a limited number of analyses per month; paid plans remove that limit.

What data we collect

  • Account data: your email address and a securely hashed version of your password (we never store your password in plain text).
  • Contract content: text or photos you submit for analysis. This is sent to Google's Gemini API to generate the analysis, and is not stored by us beyond what's needed to return your results.
  • Usage data: how many analyses you've run each month, used to enforce free-plan limits.
  • Billing data: if you upgrade to Pro, payment is handled entirely by Gumroad. We do not receive or store your card details — only your plan status and a billing reference from Gumroad.

Third-party services we use

  • Google Gemini API — processes the contract text/photos you submit to generate the analysis.
  • Gumroad — handles payment processing for Pro subscriptions.
  • Supabase — hosts our database (accounts and usage records).

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing data they process on our behalf.

Data retention & deletion

We keep your account data for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and associated data deleted, email us at the address below and we'll process the request.
